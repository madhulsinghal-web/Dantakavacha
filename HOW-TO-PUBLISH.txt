DANTAKAVACHA web app (iPhone and Android) — how to publish the link

The 7 files in this folder are the whole app. Put them on any https website; everyone then opens one link.

GitHub Pages (free)
1. github.com > + > New repository > name: dantakavacha > Public > Create.
2. "uploading an existing file" > select all files in this folder (not the folder itself) > Commit changes.
3. Settings > Pages > Source: Deploy from a branch > Branch: main, / (root) > Save.
4. After 1-2 minutes the link appears at the top of that page:
   https://YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io/dantakavacha/
5. To update later: upload the new index.html over the old one. Phones get it next time they open the app with internet.

Netlify Drop (free, even quicker)
1. On a computer, open app.netlify.com/drop and sign in (free).
2. Drag this whole folder onto the page. A link like https://something.netlify.app appears.

Note: the link is public (anyone who has it can open the app), but the app holds no data of its own;
each person's record stays only on their own phone. Use dummy data only.
